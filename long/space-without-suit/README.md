## The film in five lines

- **The want:** one selfie with the Earth for his niece Pip, without the helmet. ("You look like a goldfish," she said.)
- **The obstacle:** space itself. He presses the button marked DEPRESS, thinking it means "take the picture", and the
  airlock opens. Without the goldfish bowl his body has about fifteen seconds awake.
- **The turn:** he blacks out at fifteen; his big sister, the commander, who has been rescuing him since he was four,
  races to the airlock while the clock runs toward the edge, and slams REPRESS. The air that he wanted out of the
  picture is what has been holding him together all his life.
- **The feeling at the end:** a laugh, then wonder: we all live in a goldfish bowl, a very big and very thin one.
- **The final image:** Pip's goldfish bowl on her windowsill at night, his new selfie (two helmets, the Earth behind
  them) leaning on it; the camera pushes through the bowl's curved glass and comes out over the curve of the Earth, its
  thin blue line of air glowing, the station a bright dot crossing it; the theme resolves.

- **Motif:** the goldfish bowl. In the hook it is Pip's insult (the helmet he will not wear); in space his mouth gapes
  like a fish out of water; at the end he puts the bowl on, and Pip answers with a heart; the last shot turns her real
  goldfish bowl into the Earth's own: the thin blue line is the bowl we all live in.
- **Wordless beats:** the silence (the alarm flashing, his mouth open, no sound but his heartbeat, 1.8 s); lights out
  (the brain's city goes dark and the screen goes black); the chip bag bursting without a sound; the air roaring back
  in after REPRESS (2.2 s: fog, the sound returning, his chest heaving); the final image.
- **Colour script:** the airlock's cool white (cocky) turning red with the beacon (danger); black space and the Earth's
  blue (awe); the lung's pink and the blood's red and cyan; the brain's city of lights going dark (black); the warm
  orange corridor of his sister (hope); sepia for 1960 and 1966; the palm on deep blue; the airlock again with fog and
  white light flooding back (relief); Pip's violet bedroom and the night window (warmth); the Earth's thin blue line.
- **Score:** one theme, a little rising phrase that never lands until the end (a held breath out): stated on a music
  box in the hook, cut off by the silence of space (the score itself loses its air: no music at all for the silence
  beat); a slow ticking pulse for the seconds; glassy and thin while the oxygen leaves; it stops dead at lights out;
  a quick, urgent version on strings and drums for his sister's run; an old upright piano for 1960 and 1966; a low drone
  for the edge; nothing while the air comes back, then the theme warm on the piano; on the final image it resolves.

## Science notes

Every claim in the script, how sure science is, and where it comes from. Hedged lines are said as hedges. The research
(16 Oct 2026) checked each claim against the primary NASA and US Air Force reports below; where sources disagree, the
script says the safer thing. Almost all whole-body vacuum data come from animals (the dogs and chimps breathed pure
oxygen first), so the film says what was measured and no more.

## Rebuild

`src/build.sh` rebuilds the MP4 from scratch. It needs python3 (numpy, scipy, soundfile, pyloudnorm, pillow, certifi;
pedalboard for the studio mix chain, else the classic chain runs), node with playwright-core and a Chrome or Chromium
(`CHROME_PATH`), and ffmpeg with libx264/aac. `.claude/skills/cp-long/bin/cloud-setup.sh` installs all of it on a
fresh Ubuntu machine. The JavaScript libraries the scenes use are vendored in `src/web/vendor/` (MIT, licences
inside). Every sound effect and the whole score are synthesized in `src/audio.py` (no recorded one-shots). The
narration takes are committed in `src/voice/`, so no API call is needed unless a line of `src/script.txt`
changes. For a changed line, run `src/build.sh --synth` with `ELEVENLABS_API_KEY` set.

The word timings: ElevenLabs' alignment put a few phrases in the silence before them, so `src/realign.py` re-times
the words with whisper (faster-whisper, base.en) and `make_timeline.py` takes whisper's times for the blocks where
that puts fewer words in silence. Its result for the committed takes is `src/voice/align.json`, which `build.sh`
copies into the work folder; after `--synth` it runs `realign.py` again (and falls back to ElevenLabs' timings if
faster-whisper is missing). A full render of 4:33 (8,196 frames, 3 browsers at 400–550 ms a frame) takes about 25
minutes on 4 CPUs.
