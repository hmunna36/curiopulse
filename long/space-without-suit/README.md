## The film in five lines

- **The want:** one selfie with the Earth for his niece Pip, without the helmet. ("You look like a goldfish," she said.)
- **The obstacle:** space itself. He presses the button marked DEPRESS, thinking it means "take the picture", and the
  airlock opens. Without the goldfish bowl his body has about fifteen seconds awake.
- **The turn:** he blacks out at fifteen; his big sister, the commander, who has been rescuing him since he was four,
  races to the airlock while the clock runs toward the edge, and slams REPRESS. The air that he wanted out of the
  picture is what has been holding him together all his life.
- **The feeling at the end:** a laugh, then wonder: we all live in a goldfish bowl, a very big and very thin one.
- **The final image:** Pip's goldfish bowl on her windowsill at night, his new selfie (two helmets, the Earth behind
  them) leaning on it; the camera pushes through the bowl's curved glass and comes out over the curve of the Earth, its
  thin blue line of air glowing, the station a bright dot crossing it; the theme resolves.

- **Motif:** the goldfish bowl. In the hook it is Pip's insult (the helmet he will not wear); in space his mouth gapes
  like a fish out of water; at the end he puts the bowl on, and Pip answers with a heart; the last shot turns her real
  goldfish bowl into the Earth's own: the thin blue line is the bowl we all live in.
- **Wordless beats:** the silence (the alarm flashing, his mouth open, no sound but his heartbeat, 1.8 s); lights out
  (the brain's city goes dark and the screen goes black); the chip bag bursting without a sound; the air roaring back
