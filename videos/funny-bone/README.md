**Title:** Why Does Your FUNNY BONE Hurt So Much? ⚡

**Description:**
You bang your funny bone on a door frame and your whole arm goes ZAP. It's not funny, and it's not a bone: it's a
nerve with nothing over it but skin. Subscribe for a new strange question every day.

How it works: the ulnar nerve is one of the main nerves of your arm. It runs from your neck to your fingers, tucked
under muscle nearly all the way. The exception is your elbow, where it passes round a knob of bone in a shallow
groove, right under the skin. A knock there squashes the nerve itself against the bone, so instead of a bruise you
get an electric zing, and you feel it where that nerve ends: in your little finger and the near half of your ring
finger. The knob belongs to the bone of your upper arm, the humerus. Humerus, humorous: that may be where the name
comes from, or it may simply be the funny feeling. Nobody knows for sure. The zing fades in seconds; tingling or
numbness in those two fingers that stays is one for a doctor.

## Sound design

- **Two sounds carry it:** the knock (an elbow on wood at frame 1, a quarter of a second before "You"; later the same
  knock from inside, a heavy low thud on "squashes") and the zap (`zap` in `src/sfxkit.py`, added for this Short: a
  rough mains buzz that stutters, with crackle on top). The zap lives in the speech band, so the whole sound is heard
  only in pauses; under words only its edges run (under 250 Hz and above 5.2 kHz).
- **Beds:** the hallway (low air); inside his arm a muffled low pulse.
- **Hits:** every sound that carries a joke sits in a pause of the narration: the bang before "You", the zap after
  "ZAP!", a buzzer after "funny," and again after "bone,", a short zap after "NERVE!", a record scratch before
  "except", two knocks on bone after "bone,", a small "eep" after "skin.", a squashed toy after "it,", the zap again
  after "PINKY,", a bright note after "ends.", ba-dum tss after "Humorous." (`rimshot`, also new in sfxkit), a cricket
  after "the joke." and a low wah after "LAUGHING.". What happens ON a word is on the bed bus and is either under
  250 Hz (the basket landing, the muscles, the bone, the stamp) or above 5 kHz (the tags, the strikes, the sticker).
- **Score:** a light pizzicato groove from "bang" that stops for ZAP!; a sneaky walk (bass and snaps) under the
  answer, with holes for the two buzzers and a held chord under "it's a naked..."; a curious minor loop inside, cut by
  the scratch; a held chord under "where it runs over bone", then nothing under "right under the skin."; nothing
  under the knock; a quick bright run while the zap travels, cut off for PINKY; the sneaky walk again for "And the
  bone it gets squashed against?", and then no music at all: the pun, the rimshot, the cricket and the last line have
  the room to themselves.
- **Mix:** voice first (leveler, gentle compression), effects duck under speech, −14 LUFS, true peak ≤ −1 dBTP
  after AAC. The music bus sits 3 dB under the house level and ducks a little more (`levels={"music": -14}`,
  `duck={"music": 0.74}`): this narration has soft line endings ("skin.", "PINKY,", "ends."). `src/qa.py` on the
  delivered file: see "Ship review". `src/qc_gags.py` lists every joke sound's level in its own pause.

## Science notes

Every claim in the script, how sure science is, and where it comes from. Hedged lines are said as hedges.

| The zap lands in your pinky, "where the nerve ends" | established: the ulnar nerve gives feeling to the little finger and the little-finger side of the ring finger (the picture lights half the ring finger too); "where it ends" is the plain way of saying "in the skin it serves" | Cleveland Clinic (what the nerve feels for) |
| The bone it is squashed against is the humerus | established: the bump belongs to the lower end of the upper-arm bone | Cleveland Clinic (anatomy); Wikipedia, "Ulnar nerve" |
| "Humorous... (2019); Vagg (2013) reads the OED's first quotation, a comic poem of 1842, as the pun |

Left out on purpose: "the largest unprotected nerve in the body" (repeated everywhere, sourced thinly) and any year
for the name. In the description only: tingling or numbness that stays is one for a doctor.

The picture simplifies: the arm is drawn as an L with the palm toward us, two muscles to each half of it; the point of
the elbow (the top of the ulna) is not drawn, so the knob the nerve lies on stands for the whole joint; and nerves do
not have faces.

## Rebuild

`src/build.sh` rebuilds the MP4 from scratch in about 6 minutes. It needs python3 (numpy, scipy, soundfile,
pyloudnorm, pillow, certifi; pedalboard for the studio mix chain, else the classic chain runs), node with
playwright-core and an installed Chrome, and ffmpeg with libx264/aac. The cinematic lighting runs on the graphics
chip through Chrome's WebGL2; without one the render falls back to the classic look and says so (`look: ...`). The JavaScript libraries the scenes use are
vendored in `src/web/vendor/` (MIT, licences inside). No recorded one-shots are used in this Short.
The narration takes are committed in `src/voice/`, so no API call is needed unless a line of `src/script.txt`
changes. For a changed line, run `src/build.sh --synth` with `ELEVENLABS_API_KEY` set. `src/cover.sh` renders the
cover.
