## Sound design

- **Beds:** the dentist's room tone (under 230 Hz) and a little air above 6 kHz; inside his head the hum's own body (under
  220 Hz); the club's crowd murmur (under 260 Hz).
- **Hits:** frame 1 the mirror taps a tooth (high); his try at "Mmm" comes out as a goofy "Aaah?" in the pause before
  "Can't?"; the mirror slides out (high), his lips close (a small smack in the pause), his hum under the words (under
  300 Hz), the M's burst out on NOSE (a low thud, glints, the hitstop's crisp top) and a HONK in the pause after it; a fizz
  above 5.2 kHz on "nose buzz", a BZZT in the pause after it; the window and the dive; the lips POP open in the pause after
  "up...", a low glug as the hum spills, glints as the A's pour out, the NO M! stamp (low) and a wrong-answer buzzer after
  "instead."; the FLOSSING? bubble (high), the punch-in thud (low), his "ah-AAH!" in the pause after "dentist." with the
  hitstop's layered hit; the spotlight's clunk, the dummy's wooden jaw (above 3 kHz), the M's flipping to N's (high), a
  rimshot and the crowd's laugh after "N."; his "na-na?" in the pause after "mama...", the sweat drop, the mirror's tap as
  he opens wide (as on frame 1).
- **Score:** waiting-room bossa (104 BPM, F – Dm) for the dentist's room, Bb – C for the answer, Gm – C (no marimba) for the
  buzz, a soft drone inside his head, a 120 BPM stage vamp for the ventriloquist; the button's bossa ends on a bar line so
  frame 1's starts on one (the loop). Out for every punchline: the "Mmm" try, NOSE, the BZZT, NO M!, "dentist", "N.".
- **Mix:** voice first (leveler, gentle compression), effects duck under speech, −14 LUFS, true peak ≤ −1 dBTP after AAC.
  `src/qa.py`: see Ship review.

## Science notes

Every claim in the script, how sure science is, and where it comes from. Hedged lines are said as hedges.

| Claim | Status | Source |
|---|---|---|
| M is made with both lips closed; the soft palate (velum) is lowered, so the air and the sound leave through the nose ("M is a hum that comes out your nose") | established (phonetics: [m] is the voiced bilabial nasal stop) | UNIL, Introduction to Phonetics, nasal plosives (unil.ch/sli); UBC eNunciate, /m/ (enunciate.arts.ubc.ca/m/); Wikipedia, "Nasal consonant" |
| With the lips apart there is no bilabial closure, so no M: the hum leaves through the open mouth as a (nasalised) vowel, "aaah", or the tongue makes the closure instead ("nnng") | established (follows from the articulation above) | as above |
| Humming an M makes the nose vibrate; pinch the nose and the hum stops (the sound really goes through the nose) | established (a standard classroom demonstration) | Vaia, "Nasal sound" (the hold-your-nose test); the channel's own hum-nose-pinched Short (19 Oct 2026) |
| You can't answer your dentist with the mirror in: "mm-hmm" needs the lips shut | follows from the above (a joke, not a study) | — |
| Ventriloquists can't make a true M without moving their lips; they substitute a tongue sound, usually N (or NG), and rely on the listener hearing M | established in ventriloquism teaching (described differently by different teachers) | Scout Life, "Learn How To Be a Ventriloquist" (M -> N; B -> D; P -> T); HowStuffWorks / Stuff You Should Know, "How Ventriloquism Works" |
| "Mama" with still lips comes out "nana" | follows from the above | — |

## Rebuild

`src/build.sh` rebuilds the MP4 from scratch in about 10 minutes. It needs python3 (numpy, scipy, soundfile,
pyloudnorm, pillow, certifi; pedalboard for the studio mix chain, else the classic chain runs), node with
playwright-core and an installed Chrome, and ffmpeg with libx264/aac. The cinematic lighting runs on the graphics
chip through Chrome's WebGL2; without one the render falls back to the classic look and says so (`look: ...`). The JavaScript libraries the scenes use are
vendored in `src/web/vendor/` (MIT, licences inside). Recorded one-shots come from the repo's shared CC0 folder
`assets/sfx/` (`git sparse-checkout add assets/sfx`; credits in `assets/sfx/CREDITS.md`).
The narration takes are committed in `src/voice/`, so no API call is needed unless a line of `src/script.txt`
changes. For a changed line, run `src/build.sh --synth` with `ELEVENLABS_API_KEY` set.
