Which do you think is older? Sharks or trees? Sharks. Trees are the new kids. Rewind almost half a billion years. No trees anywhere. The tallest plant came up to your ankle, but the sea already had sharks. Trees showed up about 60 million years later, and the sharks watched. Dinosaurs came and went. Sharks?
Five mass extinctions. Still swimming? So when you hug a tree, you're hugging the new kid.
