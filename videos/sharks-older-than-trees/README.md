**The fourth CURIOUS Short** (the topic test, 25 Oct–8 Nov 2026; `reference/story.md` §6; promised by jupiter-no-ground's
"Next up" comment). The hero goes there: he hugs a big tree at the edge of the sea, eyes shut, blissful, while a shark watches
him from the water. Then the whole shore REWINDS to 450 million years ago: the forest un-grows into the ground, his own tree
slides out of his arms, he faints onto bare rock. No trees anywhere, moss up to his ankle, and the sea already full of sharks.
Then the first trees burst out of the ground, a dinosaur comes and goes, and back in today the shark pops up beside him again.
Same hero, narrator, captions and bloom look. The look is cinematic (`publish.json` "look": "cine"): a low sun over the sea
on the right in every age (`LIGHTS.sunset`, tinted per age: `L_TODAY`, `L_ORDO`, `L_DEVO`, `L_MESO` in `src/web/scenes.js`).

**A world built for the topic** (`src/web/shore.js`, `creatures.js`): one stretch of coast drawn in four ages from one state
(today: golden hour, grass, a forest and the big tree; 450 million years ago: bare rock, tide-worn slabs, moss cushions, a
purple sky; ~390 million years ago: the first trees, Wattieza-like trunks whose fern crowns unroll from fiddleheads; the
dinosaurs' time), a sea with sun glitter, soft clouds, far ridges, foam that breathes along the shore; the shark (one smug
cartoon shark through every age: brows, a toothy grin, a waving fin, shades), the water in front of it laid on as a solid
image so nothing under the surface shows; a sauropod with a walk cycle and a neck that bends down nose to nose; a fireball; a
tally rock; the NEW KID sticker; a YEARS AGO counter that rewinds and fast-forwards.

## Narration (71 words, performed)

Directed in [`src/script.txt`](src/script.txt), one take per block. The joke timing sits in the gaps between blocks.

> Which do you think is older... sharks, or TREES? / Sharks. Trees are the new kids. / Rewind almost half a billion years. /
> No trees anywhere. The tallest plant came up to your ankle. / But the sea already had sharks. / Trees showed up about sixty
> million years later... / and the sharks watched. / Dinosaurs came... and went. / Sharks? Five mass extinctions. Still
> swimming. / So when you hug a tree... you're hugging the new kid.

## Sound design

- **Beds:** the sea against the shore in every age (a slow swell under 400 Hz, foam above 5.5 kHz); a colder wind and a thin
  whistle 450 million years ago; a few birds of today, only in the pauses.
- **Hits:** the bark creaks under the hug (low), a plip when the shark tilts, a pip for the heart, glints on the grin, the
  sticker's SLAP; a tape rewinding (a chatter above 5 kHz and a low wow) under the whole rewind, a low whoosh as his tree
  un-grows, the FLOMP and its grit; a pop for the tag and a sproing for the tiny plant; the whip pan, fins cutting the water,
  a splash and a slide-whistle "hi" for the fin wave; a low thump for every first tree bursting out of the rock, a rumble, the
  fronds' rustle (top), fast-forward chatter; three blinks; the dinosaur's steps (low), a sniff nose to nose, the fireball (low
  and top only), the BOOM (low, long) and its crack (short, in the gap); five scratches for the tally (top), a glint on the
  shades; the final SPLASH and the sticker fluttering off.
- **Score:** a sunny bounce (C-F-G-C, 112 BPM) for the hug, a dark drone and a falling riser for the rewind, an empty-world
  drone with a lonely music box, a sneaky pizzicato for the sharks, a wonder swell, a riser and a crash for the first trees, a
  tuba-ish stroll for the dinosaur, a cool groove for "Still swimming", the bounce again for the button; it drops out for every
  punchline (the heart, the slap, the flomp, the sproing, the splash, "watched.", the boom, the final splash).
- **Mix:** voice first (leveler, gentle compression), effects duck under speech, −14 LUFS, true peak ≤ −1 dBTP after AAC.

  edge. Round 2: nose to nose; the rock on the land and the water kept off it; the shark turns round and cruises off.
- The water in front of a shark is an IMAGE (a solid image hides what is under it in the shape map); a see-through or clipped fill
  left the shark's underwater outline lit through the water.

## Rebuild

`src/build.sh` rebuilds the MP4 from scratch in about 10 minutes. It needs python3 (numpy, scipy, soundfile,
pyloudnorm, pillow, certifi; pedalboard for the studio mix chain, else the classic chain runs), node with
playwright-core and an installed Chrome, and ffmpeg with libx264/aac. The cinematic lighting runs on the graphics
chip through Chrome's WebGL2; without one the render falls back to the classic look and says so (`look: ...`). The JavaScript libraries the scenes use are
vendored in `src/web/vendor/` (MIT, licences inside). Recorded one-shots come from the repo's shared CC0 folder
`assets/sfx/` (`git sparse-checkout add assets/sfx`; credits in `assets/sfx/CREDITS.md`).
The narration takes are committed in `src/voice/`, so no API call is needed unless a line of `src/script.txt`
changes. For a changed line, run `src/build.sh --synth` with `ELEVENLABS_API_KEY` set.
