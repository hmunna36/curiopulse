**Spine (one sentence: you think X, but Y, because Z):** you think you're standing in a forest, but it's one tree, because every trunk is a sprout off the same roots.
**The button line, written first:** "So, tree huggers? You'll need longer arms." (runs into "This whole forest you're standing in... is ONE tree.")
**The running device and its three beats:** the trunks' eyes (aspen bark really has dark eye-shaped scars): set up, the bark eye above him peeks open and glances at him while he hugs (0.76-2.10 s); escalate, every trunk in the forest opens its eyes at him and blushes on "them ALL" (17.59 s); reverse, they all give him a flat, unimpressed look while his arms stretch round the forest (31.8-33.0 s), and the hug trunk's eye is shut again for the loop.
