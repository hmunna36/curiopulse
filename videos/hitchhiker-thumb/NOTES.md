# Can Your THUMB Bend Back Like a Hitchhiker's? 👍: direction record

How this Short meets the direction sheet (the skill's `reference/direction.md`). Write the first three lines before the
script, the rest as the Short is built. One line per rule, with the time or the frame; a rule that could not be met
says why. Keep each line's label and write after its colon (qa.py counts the answered lines; ship bar item 12).

**Spine (one sentence: you think X, but Y, because Z):** you think your thumb is one of two kinds, straight or hitchhiker's, but yours sits somewhere on a sliding scale, because a strap on the palm side of the joint stops the bend and some straps are stretchier than others.
**The button line, written first:** "Now try your OTHER thumb!" (it sends the viewer back into the test with the other hand; the two often don't match), straight into "Thumbs up!".
**The running device and its three beats:** the cars on the road: (1) a car whooshes past his straight thumb (4.8 s); (2) a car screeches to a stop for her hooked thumb (15.4 s); (3) a car finally brakes and honks for HIM when his other thumb bends further (28.4 s); for the loop the road is empty again, as on frame 0.
