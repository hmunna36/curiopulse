## Sound design

- **Beds:** rain as a low roar under 280 Hz plus a hiss and patter above 5.2 kHz (nothing in the speech band), gone while the sun
  is out; low thunder rolls at the start, during "Everyone's next to..." and into the button.
- **Hits:** fingers sliding together (skin swishes above 5.2 kHz, a soft clap under 250 Hz), each swap (out, one row over, back
  in), the lightning crack inside the 0.4 s pause before "Feels" with the thunder low under the word, the faces popping open
  (glints), a sly "hmm" slide after "over.", bliss sparkles, heads turning (a low creak), one cricket after "strangers.", the
  coin's flick (a high ting), its whirr, the THUP in the mud and three tinks as it settles, the 1 % pop, the twin's slide-in, a
  standoff "dun" after "thumbs", the 4-in-10 pops, birds in the sunbreak, the womp after "case."
- **Score:** a soft hymn pad while he prays, a sneaky snap-and-pizzicato groove for the seats (out for "one seat over" and for
  "strangers"), a warm groove for home, a low "uh-oh" drone, a light groove for the coin (out for the landing), a minor
  standoff groove for the twins (out for the glare), the uneasy hymn again, a major swell when the sun breaks through (out for
  "Just in case.").
- **Mix:** voice first (leveler, gentle compression), effects duck under speech, −14 LUFS, true peak ≤ −1 dBTP after AAC.

## Science notes

Every claim in the script, how sure science is, and where it comes from.

| Claim | Status | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Most people have a preferred way to clasp their hands, it stays consistent, and the other way feels unusual | established | Wikipedia, "Hand clasping" (citing Reiss 1999 and the survey literature) |
| Clasped fingers alternate between the hands (thumb, thumb, index, index...), so the other thumb on top moves every finger one place over, between two new neighbours | established (the geometry of an interlock) | the definition of hand clasping (Wikipedia); visible on anyone's hands |
| "It's basically a coin toss": about 55 % clasp left thumb on top, 44 % right, about 1 % have no preference; most populations are 40–75 % left | established survey data; the split varies by population (a 2014 Central European sample found right more common) | M. Reiss, "The genetics of hand-clasping: a review and a familial study", Annals of Human Biology 26:39–48 (1999); J. H. McDonald, Myths of Human Genetics, "Hand clasping"; U. S. Tran et al., Laterality 19:602–614 (2014) |
| "Even identical twins, same DNA, pick different thumbs four times in ten" | established: in four twin studies 157 of 366 identical pairs (43 %) clasp differently, about as often as fraternal pairs (131 of 299, 44 %) | Reiss 1999, tabled by McDonald, Myths of Human Genetics |
| (not said, kept out) it is "not genetic at all" | debated: family data show some resemblance (LxL parents 59 % L children, RxR 32 %), so not a simple one-gene trait, with little genetic influence by the twin data | McDonald; Reiss 1999 |
| (not said, kept out) it matches handedness | debated: older surveys found no link; Tran et al. 2014 found a weak association in 12,720 people | Tran et al. 2014 |
| The prayer and the sunshine | a joke, not a claim | — |

## Rebuild

`src/build.sh` rebuilds the MP4 from scratch in about 10 minutes. It needs python3 (numpy, scipy, soundfile,
pyloudnorm, pillow, certifi; pedalboard for the studio mix chain, else the classic chain runs), node with
playwright-core and an installed Chrome, and ffmpeg with libx264/aac. The cinematic lighting runs on the graphics
chip through Chrome's WebGL2; without one the render falls back to the classic look and says so (`look: ...`). The JavaScript libraries the scenes use are
vendored in `src/web/vendor/` (MIT, licences inside). Recorded one-shots come from the repo's shared CC0 folder
`assets/sfx/` (`git sparse-checkout add assets/sfx`; credits in `assets/sfx/CREDITS.md`).
The narration takes are committed in `src/voice/`, so no API call is needed unless a line of `src/script.txt`
changes. For a changed line, run `src/build.sh --synth` with `ELEVENLABS_API_KEY` set.
