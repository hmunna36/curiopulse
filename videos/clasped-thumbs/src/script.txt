# The test (rules of 15 Oct 2026): clasp your hands without thinking; then clasp them so the OTHER thumb is on top: it feels
# wrong. The fingers of clasped hands alternate (thumb, thumb, index, index...), so putting the other thumb on top moves
# every finger one place over, between two new neighbours. About 55 % clasp left thumb on top, 44 % right, 1 % no preference
# (Reiss 1999, Ann Hum Biol 26:39-48; most populations 40-75 % left: McDonald, Myths of Human Genetics). In four twin studies
# 157 of 366 identical pairs (43 %) clasp differently, about as often as fraternal pairs (131 of 299): little genetic
# influence (Reiss 1999, tabled by McDonald).
# Hook formula: The Direct Address, as the test (two instructions), then the answer "Feels wrong, right?".
# The answer starts by 5.0 s; up to 13 s the viewer swaps back and swaps again; the button undercuts (the hiker is praying
# for sunshine in the rain) and runs into the hook. 70 words (the short arm: 66-76).
