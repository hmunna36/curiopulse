Island in the Arctic, where the last of them lived until about 4,000 years ago. Pyramids or Moon: what did you guess?

## Sound design

- **Beds:** a very low space hum on the Moon (under 160 Hz); a desert wind at Giza and on the building site (its body
  under 400 Hz, a thin whistle above 5.8 kHz); the rush of air on the climb, swelling with his speed; the Arctic sea.
- **Hits:** the shutter (its click's top on "Nope.", the whole shutter after it), the whip to Giza, the tumbleweed's
  rustle in the pause after "history.", the rope creaking under him and his hands' dry ticks (above 5 kHz), a DING when
  each count lands (in the pauses after "years." and "thousand."), swishes for the brackets, a two-note glint for
  ~500, a BOING as he hops onto the rope, a tape-rewind warble and a rope zip down to 2560 BC, the landing thud (under
  250 Hz), the split-screen whoosh, the mammoth's TOOT (in the pause after "alive."), a sad slide after "island.", a
  little slide for the sunglasses, a glint as they go up, the bubble filling, and the gum's POP after "modern.".
- **Score:** a lo-fi selfie groove (Am F C G, 92 BPM) under the hook; a pizzicato riff in E phrygian dominant over a
  doumbek (DUM-tek) at Giza, dropping out for "ancient history."; a driving climb groove (Em C D B, 126 then 132 BPM)
  with a riser into each count and a crash on each ding; a wonder drone and a soft groove under the whole rope; a sly
  groove for the mammoth; the selfie groove back for the button, so the loop's restart is musical. The score stops for
  every punchline (Nope, ancient history, hundred years, alive / On one island, modern).
- **Mix:** voice first (leveler, gentle compression), effects duck under speech, −14 LUFS, true peak ≤ −1 dBTP after AAC.

## Rebuild

`src/build.sh` rebuilds the MP4 from scratch in about 10 minutes. It needs python3 (numpy, scipy, soundfile,
pyloudnorm, pillow, certifi; pedalboard for the studio mix chain, else the classic chain runs), node with
playwright-core and an installed Chrome, and ffmpeg with libx264/aac. The cinematic lighting runs on the graphics
chip through Chrome's WebGL2; without one the render falls back to the classic look and says so (`look: ...`). The JavaScript libraries the scenes use are
vendored in `src/web/vendor/` (MIT, licences inside). Recorded one-shots come from the repo's shared CC0 folder
`assets/sfx/` (`git sparse-checkout add assets/sfx`; credits in `assets/sfx/CREDITS.md`).
The narration takes are committed in `src/voice/`, so no API call is needed unless a line of `src/script.txt`
changes. For a changed line, run `src/build.sh --synth` with `ELEVENLABS_API_KEY` set.
