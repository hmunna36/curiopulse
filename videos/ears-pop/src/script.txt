## hook gap=0.30 tighten=0.34 tempo=1.03
[curious] Your plane takes off... and your ears go... POP!

## burp gap=0.60 tighten=0.34 tempo=1.00
[deadpan] Relax. Your ear just burped.

## pocket gap=0.30 tempo=1.02
Behind your eardrum is a tiny pocket of air.

## climb gap=0.24 tighten=0.32 tempo=1.06
As you climb, cabin pressure drops... so that air swells... until a tiny tube to the back of your nose burps the extra out.

## landing gap=0.42 tighten=0.34 tempo=1.00
[deadpan] Landing is worse.

## squeeze gap=0.20 tighten=0.30 tempo=1.06

## swallow gap=0.24 tighten=0.32 tempo=1.05
So you swallow, or yawn... and a little muscle yanks it open.

## baby gap=0.55 tighten=0.34 tempo=1.04

## button gap=0.36 tighten=0.40 tempo=1.00
