**Title:** Can You Do Fist, EDGE, Palm? ✊

**Description:**
Tap your table with a fist, then the edge of your hand, then your flat palm. Now do it FAST. Mixed them up? A fist sneaks back in where the edge should be? Your hand is fine: it's your brakes. Before each new shape, your brain has to stop the last one, and when one stop is missed, the old shape comes right back. Doctors use this exact test to check your brain. And relax: in one study, being smart didn't help. Now try it backwards.

Did you mix it up?

The science: the fist-edge-palm test (Luria's three-step test, or the Luria motor series) comes from the Soviet neuropsychologist Alexander Luria, and doctors use it at the bedside to check motor programming, the brain's ability to plan and run a sequence; it is part of the Frontal Assessment Battery (Dubois et al., Neurology, 2000). Mistakes include doing the gestures in the wrong order and repeating the same gesture (perseveration). In 72 healthy adults, a test of holding back the obvious word (the Hayling sentence completion test) predicted the mix-ups, while an intelligence test (Cattell's Culture Fair) and the Stroop test did not (Varkovetski et al., Journal of Clinical and Experimental Neuropsychology, 2020). Plenty of healthy people slip when they go fast; a mix-up on a phone screen is not a diagnosis. The STOP sign, the ninja and the goat doctor are pictures of the idea, not real parts of your brain.

## Rebuild

`src/build.sh` rebuilds the MP4 from scratch in about 10 minutes. It needs python3 (numpy, scipy, soundfile,
pyloudnorm, pillow, certifi; pedalboard for the studio mix chain, else the classic chain runs), node with
playwright-core and an installed Chrome, and ffmpeg with libx264/aac. The cinematic lighting runs on the graphics
chip through Chrome's WebGL2; without one the render falls back to the classic look and says so (`look: ...`). The JavaScript libraries the scenes use are
vendored in `src/web/vendor/` (MIT, licences inside). Recorded one-shots come from the repo's shared CC0 folder
`assets/sfx/` (`git sparse-checkout add assets/sfx`; credits in `assets/sfx/CREDITS.md`).
The narration takes are committed in `src/voice/`, so no API call is needed unless a line of `src/script.txt`
changes. For a changed line, run `src/build.sh --synth` with `ELEVENLABS_API_KEY` set.
