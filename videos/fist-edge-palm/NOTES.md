# Can You Do Fist, EDGE, Palm?: direction record

How this Short meets the direction sheet (the skill's `reference/direction.md`). Write the first three lines before the
script, the rest as the Short is built. One line per rule, with the time or the frame; a rule that could not be met
says why. Keep each line's label and write after its colon (qa.py counts the answered lines; ship bar item 12).

**Spine (one sentence: you think X, but Y, because Z):** you think tapping fist, edge, palm in order is easy, but going fast your hand slips back into the shape it just left, because your brain has to put the brakes on each shape before the next one can start.
**The button line, written first:** "Now do it... BACKWARDS." (palm, edge, fist: the viewer tries the test a new way, and the backwards run ends on the fist that frame 0 opens on).
**The running device and its three beats:** the mountain goat across the picnic table. Set up: it chews and watches, and stops chewing, one brow up, when his fist jams (4.4 s). Escalate: it turns doctor (head mirror, clipboard BRAIN TEST) and taps fist, edge, palm with its hoof, perfectly (18.6-21.7 s). Reverse: in big smart glasses it tries it fast and mixes it up, CLONK, its eyes cross (24.4-26.2 s). For the loop it is back to chewing, as on frame 0.
