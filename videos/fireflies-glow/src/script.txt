## hook gap=0.10 tempo=1.00
[excited] You catch a firefly in a jar and your whole hand glows!

## answer gap=0.50 tighten=0.70 tempo=1.00
[deadpan] That's a glow stick... with wings.

## trick gap=0.30 tempo=1.04
Same trick. Two chemicals in its tail meet oxygen... and glow.

## cold gap=0.30 tighten=0.40 tempo=1.03
No flame. Almost no heat. A light bulb would cook him.

## blink gap=0.40 tempo=1.04
[curious] The blinking? Scientists think he cuts the oxygen.

## payoff gap=0.30 tempo=1.03

## sub gap=0.35 tighten=0 tempo=1.00
[mischievously] Subscribe... it gets dark.

## fake gap=0.35 tempo=1.03
Because some females fake another species' answer. He flies down for a date...

## eat gap=0.20 tighten=0 tempo=1.00
[deadpan] and she eats him.

## taste gap=0.45 tighten=0.45 tempo=1.02

## button gap=0.50 tighten=0 tempo=1.00
