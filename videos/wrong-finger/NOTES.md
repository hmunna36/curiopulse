# Why Does the WRONG Finger Move?: direction record

How this Short meets the direction sheet (the skill's `reference/direction.md`). Write the first three lines before the
script, the rest as the Short is built. One line per rule, with the time or the frame; a rule that could not be met

**Spine (one sentence: you think X, but Y, because Z):** you think you'll wiggle the finger you're looking at, but its twin on your other hand moves, because your brain picks a hand by the side it sees it on, and crossing your wrists swapped the sides.
**The button line, written first:** "Now try it on a friend." (the viewer becomes the one who points; it runs straight into "Cross your wrists, clasp, roll them in.")
**The running device and its three beats:** the raccoon across the picnic table. Set up (3.0-5.6 s): it reaches across, points at one finger and smirks when the twin pops. Escalate (9.8-12.2 s): it points twice, fast, two more twins pop, it falls back laughing (11.9 s). Reverse (17.6-22.5 s): it TOUCHES the finger, the right one pops and its jaw drops (21.4 s); in the button its own clasped paws fail the same way and it stares at us (27.8 s). Frame 0 and the loop frames show it watching across the table, as at the start.
