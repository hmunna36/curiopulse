# Can You Move Your Eyes SMOOTHLY? 👀: direction record

How this Short meets the direction sheet (the skill's `reference/direction.md`). Write the first three lines before the
script, the rest as the Short is built. One line per rule, with the time or the frame; a rule that could not be met
says why. Keep each line's label and write after its colon (qa.py counts the answered lines; ship bar item 12).

**Spine (one sentence: you think X, but Y, because Z):** you think you can slide your eyes smoothly across a room, but they only jump, because your eyes can glide only when they have something moving to chase (even a finger you can't see: your brain feels it move).
**The button line, written first:** "Now lose the finger. Try again." It sends the viewer straight back into the test and runs into the first line ("Can you move your eyes smoothly?").
**The running device and its three beats:** the orange butterfly. Set up: on "CHASE" (9.1-10.4 s) it zips past his nose and his eyes glide after it, the first smooth move of the Short. Escalate: it lands on his fingertip on "chase it" (13.2 s) and rides it while his eyes ride the finger on two tow lines (15.1-16.8 s). Reverse: in the dark it is the one who can't see: two tiny wandering eyes that fly into his nose, BOOP (23.1 s); with the lights on it sits on his nose and flies off (24.1 s), so frame 0 has no butterfly, as at the start.
