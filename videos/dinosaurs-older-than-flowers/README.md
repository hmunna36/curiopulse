## Sound design

- **Beds:** the glade (breeze, insects, birds in the pauses: birds are dinosaurs too), the Jurassic valley (the volcano's far
  rumble, warm humid air, a dragonfly), the time road (low air, crickets in the gaps).
- **Hits:** the T. rex's sniffs and snort, petals, a ding on the question; the whip; the counter's tape rewinding (above 5 kHz,
  under the words), pops as flowers sink, a thud and ding when it lands; the Stego's footfalls, the stamp, three head-turn
  swishes, its sniff; CHOMP with a layered hitstop hit, paper crumple, munching; fast-forward, a run of sparkles for the bloom,
  the T. rex's footsteps and STOMP (layered hitstop hit, dust); the bracket glint and ding, the turn swish, a sad wah-wah for the
  far Stegosaurus; paper rustle for the offer, tiny flapping squeaks for the arms.
- **Score:** a bouncy marimba groove (F, 116 bpm) in the glade that returns for the button and the loop; a falling drone for the
  rewind; a lazy Dm groove in the Jurassic; a bright 128 bpm groove for the bloom; a dreamy pad on the time road. It drops out for
  the sniff and the CHOMP, the STOMP, "than to Stegosaurus!" and "Tiny arms."
- **Mix:** voice first (leveler, gentle compression), effects duck under speech, −14 LUFS, true peak ≤ −1 dBTP after AAC.

## Science notes

Every claim in the script, how sure science is, and where it comes from. Hedged lines are said as hedges.

| Claim | Status | Source |
|---|---|---|
| The first definite dinosaurs lived about 230 million years ago | established (Herrerasaurus, Eoraptor: Ischigualasto Formation, Argentina, ~231–228 Ma; 40Ar/39Ar 227.8 ± 0.3 Ma) | AMNH "What is the earliest known dinosaur?"; NHM "Where did dinosaurs come from?"; Sereno lab (UChicago) |
| The oldest flowers ever found are about 130 million years old | established as the oldest ACCEPTED record (late Valanginian–Hauterivian pollen, ~140–133 Ma, Italy and Israel; fossil flowers Montsechia ~130–125 Ma); molecular clocks and a disputed Middle-Triassic "angiosperm-like" pollen point earlier, so the line says "almost a hundred million years" and the tag says "*the oldest ones ever found" | Hochuli & Feist-Burkhardt 2013 (Frontiers in Plant Science, PMC3788615); Wikipedia "Fossil history of flowering plants"; Gomez et al. 2015 PNAS (Montsechia) |
| So dinosaurs are older than flowers by almost a hundred million years | follows: ~230 vs ~140–130 Ma = 90–100 million years | as above |
| Stegosaurus PROBABLY never saw a flower | leading reading: it lived ~155–145 Ma, before the oldest accepted flowers; a disputed Jurassic "flower bud" (~164 Ma, China) and Nanjinganthus are why it is hedged | NHM Dino Directory (Stegosaurus); HMNS blog "Did Dinosaurs Invent Flowers?"; Wikipedia (Nanjinganthus) |
| Its world was ferns, cycads, conifers and horsetails ("green, and more green") | established | Manitoba Museum "The plants that ruled when dinosaurs did"; Smithsonian "Flowers, pine cones and dinosaurs" |
| T. rex lived among flowers ("flowers everywhere") | established: about 90 % of the leaf species of the Hell Creek flora, where T. rex is found, are flowering plants | GSA abstracts on the Hell Creek megaflora (Johnson; North Dakota survey) |
| T. rex lived closer in time to you than to Stegosaurus | established: T. rex ~68–66 Ma, Stegosaurus ~155–145 Ma: about 80 million years apart, against about 66 million to now | Smithsonian "On Dinosaur Time"; Gizmodo "Less time separates us from T. rex than T. rex from Stego" |
| Bonus (comment and description only): the last dinosaurs met grass | established | Prasad et al., Science 310:1177 (2005): grass phytoliths in titanosaur coprolites, central India, ~65–71 Ma |

## Rebuild

`src/build.sh` rebuilds the MP4 from scratch in about 10 minutes. It needs python3 (numpy, scipy, soundfile,
pyloudnorm, pillow, certifi; pedalboard for the studio mix chain, else the classic chain runs), node with
playwright-core and an installed Chrome, and ffmpeg with libx264/aac. The cinematic lighting runs on the graphics
chip through Chrome's WebGL2; without one the render falls back to the classic look and says so (`look: ...`). The JavaScript libraries the scenes use are
vendored in `src/web/vendor/` (MIT, licences inside). Recorded one-shots come from the repo's shared CC0 folder
`assets/sfx/` (`git sparse-checkout add assets/sfx`; credits in `assets/sfx/CREDITS.md`).
The narration takes are committed in `src/voice/`, so no API call is needed unless a line of `src/script.txt`
changes. For a changed line, run `src/build.sh --synth` with `ELEVENLABS_API_KEY` set.
