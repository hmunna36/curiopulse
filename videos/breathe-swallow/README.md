Swallow... and try to breathe in at the same time. You can't. Air and spit share ONE tunnel at the back of your throat,

## Sound design

- **Beds:** wind on the summit (under 320 Hz), a muffled body tone inside (45–230 Hz), a bird in the hook's pauses,
  crickets at night (above 5 kHz).
- **Hits:** a low glug for every swallow (under 300 Hz, so it sits under words); air whooshing in above 5.2 kHz and a low
  bonk under "at the"; a full BONK in the gap before "Can't"; a squeak for HEY!; glints for the tunnel, the voice box and
  the lid; a boing (low-passed) as the voice box hops; a hard click on PAUSE and a hiss that stops dead; a low wind-up
  and the SLAM in the gap before "SHUT."; the air whooshing back; two coughs (KOFF!) in their own pauses; an alarm
  clock above "LATE"; a tick per swallow through the day; a whistle; a record scratch; the switch; one big GULP before
  "Sorry."; the swallow that frame 0 is in the middle of.
- **Score:** a light pizzicato groove for the test (C–F), a sneaky walk for the spit, a curious loop for the mechanism
  that STOPS when the brain hits pause (only a low dread under "and the lid slams"), the groove back with the air, a
  tense drone for the late lid, a quick groove with a kick for the day, and silence for the button.
- **Mix:** voice first (leveler, gentle compression), effects duck under speech, −14 LUFS, true peak ≤ −1 dBTP after
  AAC.

## Science notes

Every claim in the script, how sure science is, and where it comes from. Hedged lines are said as hedges.

| Claim | Status | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Air and food share one passage (the throat, or pharynx) before it parts into the windpipe in front and the food pipe (oesophagus) behind | established (anatomy) | standard anatomy; Cleveland Clinic, "Pharynx"; NIDCD, "Dysphagia" |
| Breathing stops during every swallow ("swallow apnea"), typically about 0.6–1 s in healthy adults; the pause is driven by the brainstem's swallowing centre, not only by the closed airway | established | Martin-Harris et al. on breathing-swallowing coordination; healthy-adult apnea ≈ 0.78 s seated (PMC7301395); 0.61 s for 10 mL swallows (Manipal tongue-hold study); range 0.5–1.5 s in respiratory-therapy teaching |
| During a swallow the voice box (larynx) rises and moves forward; you can feel the Adam's apple jump | established | hyolaryngeal elevation, standard swallowing physiology |
| The epiglottis folds down over the entrance to the windpipe while the vocal folds close; the soft palate also seals the nose | established (simplified: the vocal folds are the tighter seal; the Short shows the lid and two little doors) | standard swallowing physiology |
| "Down the wrong pipe": liquid reaches the airway before it is closed, and a cough clears it | established | aspiration / laryngeal penetration and the cough reflex |
| People swallow hundreds of times a day; one study counted about 585 a day (range 203–1,008) | established that it is hundreds; the number varies a lot between studies, so the screen says "ONE STUDY: ~600 A DAY" | Lear et al. 1965; review of spontaneous swallow frequency 2021 (PMC8481724: no consensus on a normal rate) |
| Left out on purpose: "babies can breathe and swallow at once" | disputed | (not used) |

## Rebuild

`src/build.sh` rebuilds the MP4 from scratch in about 10 minutes. It needs python3 (numpy, scipy, soundfile,
pyloudnorm, pillow, certifi; pedalboard for the studio mix chain, else the classic chain runs), node with
playwright-core and an installed Chrome, and ffmpeg with libx264/aac. The cinematic lighting runs on the graphics
chip through Chrome's WebGL2; without one the render falls back to the classic look and says so (`look: ...`). The JavaScript libraries the scenes use are
vendored in `src/web/vendor/` (MIT, licences inside). Recorded one-shots come from the repo's shared CC0 folder
`assets/sfx/` (`git sparse-checkout add assets/sfx`; credits in `assets/sfx/CREDITS.md`).
The narration takes are committed in `src/voice/`, so no API call is needed unless a line of `src/script.txt`
changes. For a changed line, run `src/build.sh --synth` with `ELEVENLABS_API_KEY` set.
