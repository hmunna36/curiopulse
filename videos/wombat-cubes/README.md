**Title:** Why Do Wombats Poop CUBES? 🟫

**Description:**
Wombats really do poop cubes, up to a hundred a day. And no, a wombat's bottom isn't square: a CT scan of a live wombat
showed it's round, like everyone else's. The corners are made inside, in the last part of the gut. Its wall has two stiff
stripes, about four times stiffer than the rest, and every squeeze the stiff parts pull in faster than the soft parts, so
the drying poop slowly gets four corners (Yang et al., Soft Matter, 2021). Why bother? Wombats leave their poop on rocks
and logs, like smelly notes for other wombats, and scientists think a cube is less likely to roll off. Would you have
eaten it? Yes or no?
The scientists who worked this out won the 2019 Ig Nobel Prize in Physics.

## Science notes

Every claim in the script, how sure science is, and where it comes from. Hedged lines are said as hedges.

| Claim | Status | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Bare-nosed wombats (*Vombatus ursinus*) poop cubes: blocks of about 2.3 × 2.5 × 4 cm with flat faces and corners (strictly rectangular prisms; the scientists call them cubes) | established | Yang, Lee, Chan … Carver, Hu, *Soft Matter* 17:475–488 (2021), §2.1 |
| "Up to a hundred a day": a wombat typically produces 80–100 of them a day, mostly above ground | established | Yang et al. 2021 (citing Triggs); Smithsonian Magazine, 28 Jan 2021 ("up to 100 cubes a day") |
| "Its butt isn't square": a CT scan of a live adult wombat (2019) showed a round anus, like other animals'; the shape is not made at the exit | established | Yang et al. 2021, §2.1 |
| "Its gut makes the corners": the cubes form inside the intestine, in its last 17 % (the distal colon); in 2018 the team put it at the last 8 % | established | Yang et al. 2021 (abstract, dissections of three road-killed wombats); The Guardian, 18 Nov 2018 |
| "Two stiff stripes": the colon's wall has two regions about twice as thick and four times as stiff, 180° apart | established (histology and tensile tests) | Yang et al. 2021, §2.2 |
| "Every squeeze, they pull in faster... and pinch corners": the stiff regions contract faster and the middles of the soft regions lag, which gives four corners over many contractions | leading idea (a 2-D model fitted to the measurements) | Yang et al. 2021, §2.3–3; Patricia Yang in Smithsonian: "The rigid and elastic parts contract at different speeds, which creates the cube shape and corners." |
| The poop is drier there (water 53 % against 81 % earlier in the gut), which helps it keep the shape | established | Yang et al. 2021, §2.1 |
| "Wombats leave poop on rocks, like notes": they leave droppings on rocks, logs and rises (latrines), thought to be messages to other wombats | generally believed | Yang et al. 2021, §1 and §3 |
| "Scientists think round notes would roll off": the flat sides may keep the droppings on those perches | hypothesis, hedged on screen and in the words; the team's dough test: on an 8° slope, cubes stopped about 20 cm closer than balls | Yang et al. 2021, §3 |
| "Factories need molds or knives for cubes": people make cubes by moulding or cutting ("We mold it, or we cut it"); the wombat gut is a new way | Yang's quote (2018); the 2021 paper also lists extrusion (which is cut to length) | Live Science, Nov 2018; Yang et al. 2021, §1 |
| Bonus (comment): the team won the 2019 Ig Nobel Prize in Physics | established | Improbable Research; LabOnline, 13 Sep 2019 |

## Rebuild

`src/build.sh` rebuilds the MP4 from scratch in about 10 minutes. It needs python3 (numpy, scipy, soundfile,
pyloudnorm, pillow, certifi; pedalboard for the studio mix chain, else the classic chain runs), node with
playwright-core and an installed Chrome, and ffmpeg with libx264/aac. The cinematic lighting runs on the graphics
chip through Chrome's WebGL2; without one the render falls back to the classic look and says so (`look: ...`). The JavaScript libraries the scenes use are
vendored in `src/web/vendor/` (MIT, licences inside). Recorded one-shots come from the repo's shared CC0 folder
`assets/sfx/` (`git sparse-checkout add assets/sfx`; credits in `assets/sfx/CREDITS.md`).
The narration takes are committed in `src/voice/`, so no API call is needed unless a line of `src/script.txt`
changes. For a changed line, run `src/build.sh --synth` with `ELEVENLABS_API_KEY` set.
