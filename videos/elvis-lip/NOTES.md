**Spine (one sentence: you think X, but Y, because Z):** you think you can curl one side of your lip up and keep that eye open, but that eye squints anyway, because the lip's lifting muscle starts right beside your eye and the order to lift spills over into it.
**The button line, written first:** "Now try the other side." (it sends the viewer back into the test with the other half of the face, and runs straight into the hook "Curl up one side of your lip...").
**The running device and its three beats:** the photo booth he is sitting in and its strip of three photos: set up, flash 1 on "LEASH" (photo 1: him squinting); escalate, flash 2 on "squinted" (photo 2: worse, both eyes nearly shut, nose shoved sideways); reverse, flash 3 in the gap before the button: the bulldog outside photobombs with a PERFECT one-sided snarl, both eyes wide open. For the loop the booth camera is back on his face with no strip in sight (frame 0).
