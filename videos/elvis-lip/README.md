## Science notes

Every claim in the script or on screen, how sure science is, and where it comes from: two independent sources each
(direction sheet, rule 7). Hedged lines are said as hedges; a myth is said as a myth.

| Claim | Status | Sources (two, independent) |
|---|---|---|
| Curl up one side of your upper lip and the eye on that side squints (narrows) in most people | established in healthy adults | Citron, Thomson, Pescarini et al., "Descriptive Study of Facial Motor Cocontractions During Voluntary Facial Movement in a Healthy Population", Facial Plast Surg Aesthet Med 2022, PMID 36083281 (89 adults; the commonest associated movement was same-side eye constriction during a snarl) · Raman, Lehn, Samuel et al., "Characterization of Snarl Types in Healthy Individuals", J Craniofac Surg 2025, PMID 40762653 (50 adults; in a one-sided snarl the same-side eyelid distances MRD1 and MRD2 fell and the same-side brow dropped, all P < 0.0001) |
| "In one study, ninety in a hundred squinted" | one study's number, said as "in one study" | Citron et al. 2022: 90.1 % of 89 healthy adults (the abstract on PubMed) · the direction of the effect is confirmed independently by Raman et al. 2025 (measured eyelid narrowing on the snarling side) |
| "That lip muscle starts right by your eye" | established anatomy | Radiopaedia, "Levator labii superioris alaeque nasalis muscle" (origin: maxilla, nasal bone and the medial canthus, the inner corner of the eye) · Wikipedia, "Levator labii superioris alaeque nasi muscle" (attached to the upper frontal process of the maxilla; inserts into the nostril and the upper lip; it elevates the upper lip, "enabling one to snarl") |
| "Doctors think the order to lift spills over into it" | leading idea (hedged: "Doctors think") | Citron et al. 2022 (motor overflow: involuntary movements accompanying voluntary ones, in stereotyped patterns, a hypothesis they put forward) · Raman et al. 2025 (a snarl in healthy people co-activates the brow and eyelid on that side; they frame it as the baseline for synkinesis, the co-activation of several muscles during one intended movement) |
| On screen: the nose wing lifts and the nose tip is pulled toward the snarling side; the brow on that side drops | established | Raman et al. 2025 (same-side alar base elevation, nasal tip deviation and brow depression in a one-sided snarl) · Wikipedia / Radiopaedia (the muscle lifts the wing of the nose and the upper lip) |
| "Blame the muscle with the LONGEST name" (LEVATOR LABII SUPERIORIS ALAEQUE NASI on the scroll) | established naming fact | Radiopaedia (the longest name of any muscle in the human body) · DocCheck Flexikon, "Musculus levator labii superioris alaeque nasi" (the longest name of all human muscles; Wikipedia gives 44 characters) |
| "Like Elvis" | a cultural reference, no claim | the one-sided lip curl is widely called the Elvis lip or Elvis sneer; the Short names it in words only (no likeness: the hiker keeps his own face and hair) |
| The leash, the glass cheek, the LIFT spark, the bulldog's perfect snarl | cartoon pictures of the claims above, no claim of their own | — |

Not said: the Elvis nickname some people give the muscle (no reliable source found), any share of people who CAN keep the eye open beyond
"ninety in a hundred" squinted, and any claim about dogs.

## Rebuild

`src/build.sh` rebuilds the MP4 from scratch in about 10 minutes. It needs python3 (numpy, scipy, soundfile,
pyloudnorm, pillow, certifi; pedalboard for the studio mix chain, else the classic chain runs), node with
playwright-core and an installed Chrome, and ffmpeg with libx264/aac. The cinematic lighting runs on the graphics
chip through Chrome's WebGL2; without one the render falls back to the classic look and says so (`look: ...`). The JavaScript libraries the scenes use are
vendored in `src/web/vendor/` (MIT, licences inside). Recorded one-shots come from the repo's shared CC0 folder
`assets/sfx/` (`git sparse-checkout add assets/sfx`; credits in `assets/sfx/CREDITS.md`).
The narration takes are committed in `src/voice/`, so no API call is needed unless a line of `src/script.txt`
changes. For a changed line, run `src/build.sh --synth` with `ELEVENLABS_API_KEY` set.
