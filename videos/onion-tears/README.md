## Science notes

Every claim in the script, how sure science is, and where it comes from.

| Claim | Status | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Cutting breaks onion cells and mixes an enzyme (alliinase, stored in the vacuole) with sulfur compounds (sulfoxides, in the cytoplasm) that are kept apart in the intact cell | established | Imai et al., "An onion enzyme that makes the eyes water", *Nature* 419, 685 (2002), https://www.nature.com/articles/419685a ; Scientific American, "What is the chemical process that causes my eyes to tear when I peel an onion?" |
| Alliinase makes a sulfur compound (1-propenesulfenic acid); a second enzyme, lachrymatory-factor synthase, turns it into the tear gas syn-propanethial-S-oxide | established (the second enzyme was discovered in 2002) | Imai et al. 2002 (above); FoodNavigator, "An end to kitchen tears" (2002) |
| The gas is volatile, reaches the eye's surface and stimulates the corneal nerve endings; the brain reflexively drives the tear glands to flush it | established | Scientific American (above); Chemistry World podcast "Propanethial S-oxide" |
| High-speed cameras show onion cutting ejects droplets in two stages; droplets can reach up to ~60 cm | established (one lab study) | Cornell: Liu, Bird, Jung et al., "Droplet outbursts from onion cutting", arXiv 2505.06016 / PNAS 2025; Physics World, "The physics behind why cutting onions makes us cry" |
| Blunter blades and faster cuts eject more (and more energetic) droplets; sharp knives and slow cuts mean fewer | established (same study) | the same |
| Not said: "chill the onion" (the popular tip). The Cornell team even suggest the fridge may not help, so the Short leaves it out. The "40× more droplets" figure in some press reports isn't in the paper, so it isn't used | — | Physics World (above) |

## Rebuild

`src/build.sh` rebuilds the MP4 from scratch in about 10 minutes. It needs python3 (numpy, scipy, soundfile,
pyloudnorm, pillow, certifi), node with playwright-core and an installed Chrome, and ffmpeg with libx264/aac.
The narration takes are committed in `src/voice/`, so no API call is needed unless a line of `src/script.txt`
changes. For a changed line, run `src/build.sh --synth` with `ELEVENLABS_API_KEY` set.
`src/make_cover.sh` renders `cover.jpg` from `SC.cover` (a one-shot copy of the timeline, frame 15).
