# The test (rules of 15 Oct 2026): bend the wrist all the way down (gently) and squeeze: the fist won't close tight;
# tip the wrist back and it clamps shut. The finger-closing muscles sit in the forearm and pull the fingers through long
# tendons that cross the wrist ("puppet strings"); with the wrist bent down they are already bunched up (slack strings),
# so they run out of pull (active insufficiency). Wrist back pulls the strings tight: the fingers close even with no
# effort (tenodesis), which is how people with paralyzed fingers (C6 tetraplegia) grab things.
# Hook formula: The Direct Address, as the test (two instructions), then the answer "Can't?".
# The answer starts by 5.0 s; the button sends them back to the test (bend it down again) and runs into the hook.
# 71 words (the short arm: 66-76).

## hook gap=0.03 tighten=0.15 tempo=1.04
