lemonade straw in his mouth, while the Sun blinks out of existence in a window on space and nothing happens to him...

- **Beds:** the sea on the beach (slow swells breaking every ~5.6 s, a whole number of them in the Short, the body under 600 Hz)
  and gulls in the beach shots' longer silences; the same sea darker and quieter in the night; a low breathing hum in space.
- **Hits:** the window's pop-up, the switch's click in the gap before "vanished", the Sun's POP (a low "bwup" and a top fizz:
  it can sit on the word), a sip at frame 1, the countdown's slam on "EIGHT" (low) and its tick (top only, twice a second),
  envelope swishes and glints, the postmark THUD, the cut to space, the long travel down the road, LAST (a glint), the rope's
  hum, the ring's whoom, the wind and the tumbleweed in the pause after "nothing.", three countdown beeps between "Then..."
  and "lights", the breaker CHUNK on "out" and the power dying in the gap, the rope's twang, Earth's fly-by, an ice-cube
  clink after "feel it.", a doubtful low "hmm" slide after "Probably.".
- **Score:** a carefree beach groove (C Am F G, 100 BPM, snaps, no kick) that keeps going after the Sun pops (he doesn't
  know); a wondering arpeggio and drone in space; a minor tension groove for the pull; a held drone and a riser into the

- **Mix:** voice first (leveler, gentle compression), effects duck under speech, −14 LUFS, true peak ≤ −1 dBTP
  after AAC.

## Science notes

Every claim in the script, how sure science is, and where it comes from.

| Claim | Status | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Sunlight takes about 8 minutes 20 seconds to reach Earth (average distance 150 million km; 499 s for 1 AU; it varies from about 8:10 to 8:27 over the year) | established | Smithsonian National Air and Space Museum, "How long does it take light from the Sun to get to Earth?" (howthingsfly.si.edu); IAU definition of the astronomical unit (149,597,870.7 km) / c |
| So if the Sun vanished, the sunlight already on its way would keep arriving for about 8 minutes: you would keep sunbathing | established (a thought experiment: the Sun cannot really vanish) | C. Baird, West Texas A&M University, "What is the speed of gravity?" (2013): Earth's sky would go dark about eight and a half minutes later |
| Changes in gravity travel at the speed of light, so Earth would also keep orbiting the empty spot for those 8 minutes | established | Baird (above); LIGO/Virgo, Fermi and INTEGRAL, GW170817 and GRB 170817A (ApJL 848 L13, 2017): gravitational waves and gamma rays from a neutron-star merger 130 million light-years away arrived 1.7 s apart, so gravity's speed equals light's to within about one part in 10^15 |
| Then Earth would fly off in a straight line, at about 30 km/s | established (ignoring the other planets' small pulls) | BBC Science Focus, "What would happen to Earth's orbit if the Sun vanished?": "in a straight line off into space... at about 30km/s"; NASA Earth fact sheet: mean orbital speed 29.78 km/s |
| You wouldn't feel it | established | Earth and everything on it are in free fall round the Sun; switching the Sun's pull off changes the motion of all of it together, and you only feel changes of motion relative to what is around you (Caltech IPAC Cool Cosmos, "Why don't we feel Earth move?"; NRAO, "Why can't we feel the Earth's motion around the Sun?"). The solar part of the tides would change, slowly. |

## Rebuild

`src/build.sh` rebuilds the MP4 from scratch in about 10 minutes. It needs python3 (numpy, scipy, soundfile,
pyloudnorm, pillow, certifi; pedalboard for the studio mix chain, else the classic chain runs), node with
playwright-core and an installed Chrome, and ffmpeg with libx264/aac. The cinematic lighting runs on the graphics
chip through Chrome's WebGL2; without one the render falls back to the classic look and says so (`look: ...`). The JavaScript libraries the scenes use are
vendored in `src/web/vendor/` (MIT, licences inside). Recorded one-shots come from the repo's shared CC0 folder
`assets/sfx/` (`git sparse-checkout add assets/sfx`; credits in `assets/sfx/CREDITS.md`).
The narration takes are committed in `src/voice/`, so no API call is needed unless a line of `src/script.txt`
changes. For a changed line, run `src/build.sh --synth` with `ELEVENLABS_API_KEY` set.
