# The test (rules of 15 Oct 2026): snap; then snap with the thumb and middle finger barely touching: it fizzles. A snap is
# a latch-and-spring move (Acharya, Challita, Ilton & Bhamla, J R Soc Interface 2021): the thumb presses on the middle
# finger, skin friction holds it (the latch) while the muscles load it, then it slips and the finger whips into the palm in
# ~7 ms; the pop is the finger hitting the palm. Barely touching = almost nothing loaded, so it fizzles. Squishy fingertip
# pads grip better (more contact); metal thimbles cut the snap's speed ~3.4x (rubber ~2.2x, lubricant ~4x). Peak angular
# acceleration ~1.6 million deg/s^2: the highest measured in a human, ~2.6x a pitcher's arm ("almost triple").
# Hook formula: The Direct Address, as the test (two instructions), then the answer "Fizzled?".
# The answer starts by 5.0 s; the button sends them back to the test (a slow snap: impossible) and runs into the hook.
# 69 words (the short arm: 66-76).
