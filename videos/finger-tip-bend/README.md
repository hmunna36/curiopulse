   the last three; candidates: The Question "Can you bend just the tip of your finger? Try it." (it asks instead of
   making them do it), The Impossible Claim "Your fingertip can't move on its own." (false for most once the middle is

## Sound design

- **Beds:** the cafe's room tone under 230 Hz; the wood stove's crackle above 5.4 kHz.
- **Hits:** the strain (a low creak under the words), the BOING of the middle joint in the pause after "TIP.", the ring's
  glint, the menu card's flap, the COMBO ONLY stamp (a low thud), the cash register CHA-CHING after "COMBO." and again
  after "the combo's back." (the callback), the glass (glints), the string's pluck, the puller's creak, the TWANG, his
  other hand taking hold (a soft low pat), the marimba ta-da in the pause where the viewer tries the second test, IT BENDS!
  (a low pop), the head mirror's ting, a party horn after "Congrats.", the stamp, the hand letting go (top only).
- **Score:** a light marimba groove (108 BPM) in short sections that stop for every punchline (the BOING, COMBO, the
  ta-da, "Congrats.", the end); a low drone inside the glass hand.
- **Mix:** voice first (leveler, gentle compression), effects duck under speech, −14 LUFS, true peak ≤ −1 dBTP
  after AAC. `src/qa.py`: 20/20 in round 3: voice clear (mean 24.7 dB in the speech band, min 4.8 dB), WER 1.7 %, -14.02 LUFS, -1.86 dBTP.

## Science notes

Every claim in the script, how sure science is, and where it comes from. Hedged lines are said as hedges.

| Claim | Status | Source |
|---|---|---|
| One tendon (flexor digitorum profundus, the "deep" one) bends the fingertip; it starts at a muscle in the forearm and attaches to the last bone | established | ASSH, [What is a flexor tendon injury?](https://assh.org/handcare/blog/what-is-a-flexor-tendon-injury); AAOS OrthoInfo, [Flexor tendon injuries](https://www.orthoinfo.org/diseases--conditions/flexor-tendon-injuries/); [WikiMSK: Flexor digitorum profundus](https://wikimsk.org/wiki/Flexor_Digitorum_Profundus) |
| It runs along the palm side across the middle joint (and the knuckle), so when it pulls it bends the middle joint too ("It pulls the tip AND the middle joint") | established | WikiMSK (FDP flexes the end joint and helps flex the middle joint and the knuckle); OrthoInfo |
| Hold the middle joint straight and the tip bends on its own: this is the clinical FDP test ("That's exactly how hand doctors check that string") | established | Hand Surgery Resource, [Muscle test: FDP](https://www.handsurgeryresource.net/muscle-test-fdp); RCEM Learning, [Tendon evaluation tests](https://www.rcemlearning.co.uk/modules/soft-tissue-injuries-of-the-hand/lessons/flexor-tendon-injuries/topic/tendon-evaluation-tests/) |
| Many people's middle joint bends too when they try to bend only the tip | common experience, no figure found: said as a question ("Middle joint bent too?"), never as a share | the queue's note; [Straight Dope thread](https://boards.straightdope.com/t/can-you-bend-just-the-last-joint-of-your-fingers/200240) (some people can; anecdotal) |
| A small side ligament (the oblique retinacular ligament, Landsmeer's) may also link the two joints | debated: not said | Landsmeer's tenodesis idea vs. Harris & Kaplan; [Anatomy of Landsmeer ligaments, redefined (2019)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31602135/); [Borgström et al. 2024](https://lup.lub.lu.se/record/0ffdd226-c402-453e-a3e1-9ed9bc420545) |

The "string" is the tendon, the "puller" the forearm muscle: everyday words, no science word spoken.

## Rebuild

`src/build.sh` rebuilds the MP4 from scratch in about 10 minutes. It needs python3 (numpy, scipy, soundfile,
pyloudnorm, pillow, certifi; pedalboard for the studio mix chain, else the classic chain runs), node with
playwright-core and an installed Chrome, and ffmpeg with libx264/aac. The cinematic lighting runs on the graphics
chip through Chrome's WebGL2; without one the render falls back to the classic look and says so (`look: ...`). The JavaScript libraries the scenes use are
vendored in `src/web/vendor/` (MIT, licences inside). Recorded one-shots come from the repo's shared CC0 folder
`assets/sfx/` (`git sparse-checkout add assets/sfx`; credits in `assets/sfx/CREDITS.md`).
The narration takes are committed in `src/voice/`, so no API call is needed unless a line of `src/script.txt`
changes. For a changed line, run `src/build.sh --synth` with `ELEVENLABS_API_KEY` set.
