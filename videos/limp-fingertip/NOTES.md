# Hook Your Finger. Can You Straighten the TIP? 👆: direction record

How this Short meets the direction sheet (the skill's `reference/direction.md`). Write the first three lines before the
script, the rest as the Short is built. One line per rule, with the time or the frame; a rule that could not be met

**Spine (one sentence: you think X, but Y, because Z):** you think you can straighten your fingertip whenever you like, but with the middle knuckle bent it just hangs limp, because bending that knuckle pulls the middle string tight and leaves the tip's two strings slack.
**The button line, written first:** "Still floppy? Congrats... you're FINE." (the sting after the scary line: a limp tip is the healthy result; it sends the viewer back to their own floppy tip and runs into "Bend your finger like a hook...").
**The running device and its three beats:** the ladybug on his fingertip: set up (frame 0: she sits on the hooked tip, which cannot lift even her), escalate (the pokes bounce her like a trampoline, then she climbs into the slack string and naps in it like a hammock, and puts on a doctor's head mirror and taps the tip with a reflex hammer), reverse (the snap: the strings yank taut and fling her over the lake); she floats back down and lands on the tip for the loop, as on frame 0.
