# Can Your Hands Tap Two DIFFERENT Beats? 🥁: direction record

How this Short meets the direction sheet (the skill's `reference/direction.md`). Write the first three lines before the
script, the rest as the Short is built. One line per rule, with the time or the frame; a rule that could not be met
says why. Keep each line's label and write after its colon (qa.py counts the answered lines; ship bar item 12).

**Spine (one sentence: you think X, but Y, because Z):** you think each hand can keep its own beat, but two against three glues into one, because your hands share one timer (one drummer) that only does easy counts, so drummers cheat by turning both beats into ONE rhythm: "nice cup of tea".
**The button line, written first:** "Now you. Nice... cup of tea." (the trick handed to the viewer: it sends them straight back to the test with a new way to do it, and runs into "Tap two with your left hand, three with your right. GO!").
**The running device and its three beats:** the kettle with a face on the camp stove across the table: set up (frame 0 on: it watches his hands and quietly hops its lid in the NICE-CUP-OF-TEA rhythm all along), escalate (while the drummer panics and he speeds up it boils over: shaking, steam, its lid clattering, eyes wide, 11.98-16.71 s), reverse (on TEA it whistles, WHEEE, and pours his tea: 23.06 s); back to watching his hands, lid hopping, for the loop.
