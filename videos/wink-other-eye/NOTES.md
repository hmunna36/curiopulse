How this Short meets the direction sheet (the skill's `reference/direction.md`). Write the first three lines before the

**Spine (one sentence: you think X, but Y, because Z):** you think a wink shuts one eye, but your other eye squints too, because a blink pulls both lids on one string and a wink means holding one back, and the hold slips.
**The button line, written first:** "Now try the OTHER eye." (back into the test, the other eye; it runs into "Wink. Keep your other eye STILL.")
**The running device and its three beats:** the cat spy at the next table in a fedora, waiting for his wink signal: set up (tilts its head at his double squint, ots 3.1-4.5 s), escalate (loses patience: taps the table and checks its watch, wink shot 12.5-14.6 s), reverse (winks back, and ITS other eye squints too, catwink 22.5-24.3 s); at its table again for the loop.
