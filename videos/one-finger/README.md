## Science notes

Every claim in the script, how sure science is, and where it comes from. Hedged lines are said as hedges.

| Claim | Status | Source |
|---|---|---|
| One forearm muscle lifts the index, middle, ring and little fingers through four cords (tendons) over the back of the hand; the index and little fingers also have private lifters | established | standard anatomy of the extensor digitorum, extensor indicis and extensor digiti minimi |
| Bands on the back of the hand (juncturae tendinum) tie the neighbouring cords together, just behind the knuckles | established | von Schroeder & Botte, J Hand Surg Am 1995; meta-analysis of 19 cadaveric studies (2,060 hands): PMC4606223 |
| The ring finger's cord is tied to BOTH neighbours in most hands: a band in the 3rd space (middle–ring; type 2 in ~59 % in von Schroeder's data) and in the 4th (ring–little; present in 92 % of 50 hands, mostly the thick type 3) | established (frequencies vary by study and ancestry) | von Schroeder & Botte 1995 via JCDR 2015 (jcdr.net/ReadXMLFile.aspx?id=6442); Folia Morphologica 2013 (FM.2013.0040) |
| So raising the middle finger drags the ring finger up, and the ring finger alone is the hardest to move on its own (the "handcuffs" are a picture of those bands; the brain moving the fingers as a team adds to it) | established | Häger-Ross & Schieber, J Neurosci 2000 (ring finger least individuated, thumb and index most) |
| "A perfect one takes practice" | a light line: individuation improves with practice (pianists), within the limit of the bands | general; not a claim with a number |
| Robert Schumann developed an apparatus to strengthen his middle finger ("Cigarrenmechanik"); by 1831 his diary called the third (middle) finger "completely stiff"; a doctor found "a complete palsy of the third finger of the right hand and partial of the index finger"; he never went back to concert playing | documented (diaries, letters, doctors' reports) | Hektoen International, "Robert Schumann's hand injury" (2020); Wikipedia "Robert Schumann" (paralysis in at least one finger of the right hand; by 1832 a virtuoso career was impossible) |
| Whether the gadget caused it: disputed (focal dystonia, a nerve problem, mercury treatment and a tendon problem have all been proposed; Clara Schumann said it was not caused by such a device) | debated: said as a hedge ("The gadget's fault? Historians still argue.") | Eric Sams, "Schumann's Hand Injury" (Musical Times); Hektoen 2020 |

## Rebuild

`src/build.sh` rebuilds the MP4 from scratch in about 10 minutes. It needs python3 (numpy, scipy, soundfile,
pyloudnorm, pillow, certifi; pedalboard for the studio mix chain, else the classic chain runs), node with
playwright-core and an installed Chrome, and ffmpeg with libx264/aac. The cinematic lighting runs on the graphics
chip through Chrome's WebGL2; without one the render falls back to the classic look and says so (`look: ...`). The JavaScript libraries the scenes use are
vendored in `src/web/vendor/` (MIT, licences inside). Recorded one-shots come from the repo's shared CC0 folder
`assets/sfx/` (`git sparse-checkout add assets/sfx`; credits in `assets/sfx/CREDITS.md`).
The narration takes are committed in `src/voice/`, so no API call is needed unless a line of `src/script.txt`
changes. For a changed line, run `src/build.sh --synth` with `ELEVENLABS_API_KEY` set.
