# The test: say "spoon" out loud, fast, over and over: after about thirty goes the sound stays but the meaning drops
# away (semantic satiation). Tian & Huber 2010 (Cognitive Psychology 60:267-290): the slowing comes when the SAME word is
# used again and again to reach its meaning; the word itself and the meaning itself are not what tires: it is the link
# between them. Moulin et al. 2021 (Memory 29:933-942; 2023 Ig Nobel Prize for Literature): 94 students copied words
# like "door" over and over; 66 of 93 (70.9 %) stopped at least once because the word felt peculiar, after about 33
# copies (about a minute).
# Hook formula: The Impossible Claim ("You can break a word."), then the test as an instruction. The chant itself is
