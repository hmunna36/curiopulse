A CURIOUS Short (the Curious queue's first line, promised by eiffel-summer's "Next up"), on the tight arm (25–30 s, the rules of
5 Nov 2026), framed as a comparison of time: the honey in your kitchen outlives you, and the oldest honey ever found is older than the
pyramids. The hiker eats breakfast in a new kitchen world, shrinks into the jar, where the honey is a golden desert, grows old in a
time-lapse beside a jar that never changes, then digs up the oldest honey in a torch-lit tomb.
The look is the cinematic one (`publish.json` "look": "cine"): the kitchen is lit from its window on the left (the house rig), the
inside of the jar by the lamp-sun on the right (`HN_L_JAR`), the tomb by torches (`LIGHTS.lanterns`), the pyramids by a low Sun on the
right (`LIGHTS.sunset`). The cinema kit plans the camera: 3 whips, 3 punch-ins, a tilt, moods day → warm → day → dusk → warm → dusk → day.

- **Hits:** glints as the camera lands on each breakfast item; the jar's TING after "Honey."; whips into the jar, the tomb and the

  his back; the lid's clink, thread and CLUNK; the time-lapse's ticks and tearing pages speeding up, flies, then a tape stop; the jar's

  as the crust lifts; a fly's bzzz in the last gap.

  in D minor under the slurp; the breakfast groove at 168 bpm in sixteenths for the time-lapse; low drones in the tomb and at dusk. It
  drops out for every punchline: "Honey.", "DESERT", the last slurp, the tape stop, "found?", the fanfare and the button.
- **Mix:** voice first (leveler, gentle compression), effects duck under speech, −14 LUFS, true peak ≤ −1 dBTP after AAC. Every gag

## Science notes

Every claim in the script, how sure science is, and where it comes from. Hedged lines are said as hedges.

| Claim | Status | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Honey is a "desert" to germs: too little free water for them to grow; its sugar draws water out of microbes (osmosis) | established (water activity ~0.56–0.62, below the ~0.9 most bacteria need; reviews add that osmosis is not the only factor) | Smithsonian Magazine, "The Science Behind Honey's Eternal Shelf Life" (Amina Harris, UC Davis Honey and Pollination Center); Almasaudi 2021, "The antibacterial activities of honey", Saudi J Biol Sci (PMC8071826); Bristol chemistry pages on honey as an antimicrobial |
| Honey is acidic and makes a little hydrogen peroxide (glucose oxidase) | established (in the description only) | Smithsonian Magazine (as above); National Honey Board (pH ~3.9) |
| Sealed, honey can stay good for centuries | established as stated by the industry body, "can" (it darkens or crystallises; quality changes, safety does not) | National Honey Board FAQ, honey.com/faq: "honey stored in sealed containers can remain stable for decades and even centuries" |
| The oldest honey ever found is older than the pyramids | reported, hedged in the description: honey traces (pollen) on the walls of clay jars from a ~5,500-year-old tomb near Sakire, Borjomi, Georgia (excavated 2003, reported 2012) | Eurasianet, "Report: Georgia Unearths the World's Oldest Honey" (G. Lomsadze, 30 Mar 2012); the Great Pyramid ~2560 BCE (~4,500 years) |
| Edible honey from Egyptian tombs | myth / unverified: NOT said; the description says the story has no clear source | Auckland Beekeepers Club, "The Shelf Life of Honey: A Sticky Myth Uncovered"; Tutankhamun's jars' residues were never confirmed as honey |
| Babies under 1 should not eat honey | established (description and the "Next up" comment) | CDC / NHS infant botulism guidance |
| 2,500-year-old honey residue in Paestum (bonus, not in the Short) | established (JACS 2025, doi 10.1021/jacs.5c04888) | University of Oxford news, 30 Jul 2025 |

## Rebuild

`src/build.sh` rebuilds the MP4 from scratch in about 10 minutes. It needs python3 (numpy, scipy, soundfile,
pyloudnorm, pillow, certifi; pedalboard for the studio mix chain, else the classic chain runs), node with
playwright-core and an installed Chrome, and ffmpeg with libx264/aac. The cinematic lighting runs on the graphics
chip through Chrome's WebGL2; without one the render falls back to the classic look and says so (`look: ...`). The JavaScript libraries the scenes use are
vendored in `src/web/vendor/` (MIT, licences inside). Recorded one-shots come from the repo's shared CC0 folder
`assets/sfx/` (`git sparse-checkout add assets/sfx`; credits in `assets/sfx/CREDITS.md`).
The narration takes are committed in `src/voice/`, so no API call is needed unless a line of `src/script.txt`
changes. For a changed line, run `src/build.sh --synth` with `ELEVENLABS_API_KEY` set.
