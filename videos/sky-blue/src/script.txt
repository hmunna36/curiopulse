## hook gap=0.06 tempo=1.03
You gulp your water under a blue sky that should be black.

## answer gap=0.45 tighten=0 tempo=1.00
It's only blue because sunlight keeps crashing into the air.

## colours gap=0.32 tighten=0.50 tempo=1.05
[curious] Sunlight is every colour at once. Red rolls in on long, lazy waves... and slips right past the air.

## blue gap=0.22 tighten=0.28 tempo=1.05
[excited] Blue comes in short, twitchy waves. It smacks into air molecules... and bounces everywhere.

## sky gap=0.30 tempo=1.02
So blue hits your eyes from every direction. That's your sky.

## moon gap=0.35 tighten=0.16 tempo=1.04
No air, no blue: on the Moon, the sky is black at noon.

## sunset gap=0.60 tighten=0.24 tempo=1.05
At sunset, the light crosses nearly forty times more air. The blue's all bounced away... and you get the leftovers.

## mars gap=0.40 tighten=0.26 tempo=1.00
[mischievously] On Mars? Backwards. Butterscotch sky... blue sunset. Blame the dust.

## button gap=0.70 tighten=0 tempo=1.00
