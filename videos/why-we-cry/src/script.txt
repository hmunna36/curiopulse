## hook gap=0.10 tempo=1.00
You're eating ice cream at a sad movie and your eyes spring a leak!

## answer gap=0.50 tighten=0.60 tempo=1.00
Relax, scientists think your face just called for backup.

## alarm gap=0.50 tempo=1.00
Big feelings trip an alarm in your brain, and it opens a tap above each eye.

## drain gap=0.40 tighten=0.50 tempo=1.00

## spill gap=1.10 tempo=1.00

## proof gap=0.50 tighten=0.50 tempo=1.00

## only gap=0.70 tempo=1.00
