# The test (rules of 15 Oct and 5 Nov 2026): a duck hand, the big knuckles bent all the way, the fingers straight; now try
# to spread the fingers: they barely move. Flatten the hand and they spread at once. The side straps of each big knuckle
# (the collateral ligaments of the MCP joint) start on the back of a bone end that bulges toward the palm (the cam-shaped
# metacarpal head), so they are slack with the finger straight and stretched tight with the knuckle bent: no sideways
# wiggle. Doctors splint injured hands with the big knuckles bent 70-90 degrees and the fingers straight (the "position of
# safety") so those straps stay long and cannot shorten and stiffen the knuckles.
# Hook formula: The Direct Address, as the test (the instruction itself), then "Stuck?". The answer starts by 5.0 s;
# the button sends them back to the test (duck again) and runs into the hook. 62 words (the tight arm: 55-64).
