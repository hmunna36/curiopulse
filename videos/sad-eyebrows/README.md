**Title:** Can You Make SAD Eyebrows on Purpose? 🥺

**Description:**
Make your eyebrows sad. Lift only the inner ends. Look surprised? Your sad eyebrows are locked. Each brow has two ropes: one pulls UP, one pulls IN. Sad pulls both at once, and your brain mostly saves that for real sadness. Dogs? They have an extra muscle just for this, and the best puppy eyes get adopted faster. Now try again... picture a wet puppy!

Sad or surprised?

The science: the sad brow lifts only the inner ends of the eyebrows (the middle of the forehead muscle) while the frowning muscles pull them together. Charles Darwin called the pair the 'grief-muscles' and wrote that few people can work them on purpose without practice, though many manage after repeated tries (The Expression of the Emotions in Man and Animals, 1872). Paul Ekman calls the angled-up inner brows one of the most reliable signs of sadness, because few people can move those muscles at will. Dogs have a muscle that raises the inner eyebrow (levator anguli oculi medialis); in a 2019 dissection study it was present in almost every dog and almost absent in wolves (Kaminski et al., PNAS 2019), and shelter dogs that made the inner-brow raise more often were rehomed sooner (Waller et al., PLOS ONE 2013).

| Claim | Status | Sources (two, independent) |
|---|---|---|
| The sad brow lifts only the inner ends of the brows (the middle of the forehead muscle) while the frowning muscles pull them in; the two pull against each other | established | Darwin, *The Expression of the Emotions in Man and Animals* (1872), ch. ## Rebuild

`src/build.sh` rebuilds the MP4 from scratch in about 10 minutes. It needs python3 (numpy, scipy, soundfile,
pyloudnorm, pillow, certifi; pedalboard for the studio mix chain, else the classic chain runs), node with
playwright-core and an installed Chrome, and ffmpeg with libx264/aac. The cinematic lighting runs on the graphics
chip through Chrome's WebGL2; without one the render falls back to the classic look and says so (`look: ...`). The JavaScript libraries the scenes use are
vendored in `src/web/vendor/` (MIT, licences inside). Recorded one-shots come from the repo's shared CC0 folder
`assets/sfx/` (`git sparse-checkout add assets/sfx`; credits in `assets/sfx/CREDITS.md`).
The narration takes are committed in `src/voice/`, so no API call is needed unless a line of `src/script.txt`
changes. For a changed line, run `src/build.sh --synth` with `ELEVENLABS_API_KEY` set.
